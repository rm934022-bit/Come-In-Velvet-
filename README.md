# Come In, Velvet! 🎃🦇
**A Spooky-Cute 2D Gatekeeper Platformer — Theme: *"Uninvited"***

👉 **Play Live in Your Browser:** [**https://come-in-velvet.vercel.app/**](https://come-in-velvet.vercel.app/)

You play as **Velvet**, the party’s vampire-witch gatekeeper, and your job is to keep your haunted mansion party under control before **12:00 AM Midnight**!

## 🎮 What's the Gameplay About?
- 🕵️ **Question Guests Step-by-Step**: Interrogate visitors at the front door (`E` then `SPACE` / `1` `2` `3`). Check their invitation ticket codes, ask the stage's secret password, and scan their costumes & footwear!
- 🚪 **Identify Uninvited Visitors**: Spot disguised impostors at the front gate — and watch out for sneaky crashers climbing through the **2nd-Floor Balcony Window** or dropping down the **3rd-Floor Attic Chimney**!
- 🦇 **Bat Form & Multi-Floor Platformer Chases**: Transform into a flying **Vampire Bat** (`SHIFT` / `K`) and use **Sonic Swoop** (`J` / `Click`) across 3 stories of platforms, bounce pads, warp mirrors, wind streams, and chandeliers to catch fleeing gatecrashers before they escape!
- ⏰ **Protect Your Home Until Midnight**: Security rules tighten at **10:00 PM** and **11:00 PM**. Keep **Party Control** above `0%` until the clock chimes **12:00 AM**!

## 🏰 3 Playable Manor Stages
1. **Stage 1 — The Grand Ballroom** (`LVL 1: GRAND BALLROOM`)
   - **Gimmicks**: Swinging Chandeliers & Velvet Bounce Ottomans.
   - **Security Rules**: Password `"BLOOD MOON"` • Tickets must start with `VV-` and end in an **EVEN** digit.
2. **Stage 2 — Haunted Alchemy Conservatory** (`LVL 2: ALCHEMY LAB`)
   - **Gimmicks**: Moving Levitating Shelves, Haunted Warp Mirrors (`WARP A` ↔ `WARP B`), and Cauldron Steam Vents.
   - **Security Rules**: Password `"WITCHING HOUR"` • Tickets must start with `AL-` and end in an **ODD** digit.
3. **Stage 3 — Clockwork Belfry Rooftops** (`LVL 3: CLOCKWORK BELFRY`)
   - **Gimmicks**: Updraft Wind Streams (`▲ WIND ▲`), Oscillating Brass Cog Platforms, Rotating Clockwork Gears, and Diagonal Bell-Tower Warp Mirrors.
   - **Security Rules**: Password `"NIGHTSHADE"` • Tickets must start with `CW-` and end in a **MULTIPLE OF 3**.

## 🕹️ Controls
| Key / Input | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| `A` / `D` or `◀` / `▶` | Move Left / Right |
| `W` / `SPACE` / `▲` | Jump & Double Jump |
| `S` / `▼` | Drop Through One-Way Platforms |
| `E` | Question Guest at Front Door |
| `SPACE` / `1` `2` `3` *(in Door Check)* | Ask Questions One-by-One |
| `F` / `X` *(in Door Check)* | **Admit Guest** / **Uninvited!** (Reject & Chase) |
| `SHIFT` or `K` | Toggle **Vampire Bat Form** 🦇 |
| `J` or `Left Click` | **Sonic Swoop Dash** / Catch Gatecrasher |
| `TAB` or `G` | Toggle Velvet's Gatekeeper Ledger & VIP Roster |
| `ESC` or `P` | Pause Menu / Stage Selector |
| `M` | Toggle Procedural Chiptune Music & SFX |

## 🚀 Play Online or Run Locally
- **Play Online**: [https://come-in-velvet.vercel.app/](https://come-in-velvet.vercel.app/)
